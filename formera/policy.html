Privacy Policy

Overview

Formera is a workshop app for people who sell 3D prints. It tracks filament spools, print history, products, costs and sales at fairs. This policy explains what data Formera stores, which services process it, and how you can delete it.

Demo Workspace

The demo workspace runs on sample data on your device. It does not create an account and does not send your data to our servers.

Data We Collect

When you sign in and use your own workshop, Formera stores:

  • Account information: email address and sign-in identifiers from Apple, Google or email codes
  • Workshop settings: name, language, time zone, currency and pricing tariffs
  • Filament spools: brand, material, color, weight, cost and status
  • Print history: job names, dates, durations, material usage, plate thumbnails and your color and fraction corrections
  • Products, recommended prices, fairs, reserved stock and recorded sales
  • Bambu Lab connection data: an access token for Bambu Cloud and, if you add them, printer LAN details (serial number, IP address, access code)
  • Workshop assistant questions and answers
  • Purchase status of Formera Pro and its product identifier

Formera does not use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics.

How Data Is Stored

Formera uses Supabase for sign-in, the database, private file storage and backend functions. Row Level Security limits each account to its own workshop. Bambu tokens and printer LAN details are encrypted on the server before they are stored. To keep fair sales working without a connection, recent workshop data and queued sales are also kept on your device.

Bambu Lab

When you connect Bambu Cloud, you sign in with a code that Bambu Lab sends to your email. Formera's backend then uses the resulting token to read your printers and print history from Bambu Lab's servers. Formera does not store your Bambu password. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the stored token.

Workshop Assistant

The workshop assistant answers questions about your workshop. When you send a question, Formera's backend sends it to Anthropic together with recent messages from the same conversation, your pricing tariffs and your filament inventory, so the answer can use your numbers. The app shows this before you send your first message. Anthropic processes this data to generate the answer and does not use it to train its models. Conversations are stored in your account and are deleted with it.

Purchases

Formera Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Formera uses RevenueCat to verify the purchase and link it to your account using an anonymous account identifier. We do not receive your payment details.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase for sign-in, database, private storage and backend functions
  • Bambu Lab for printer and print history, when you connect it
  • Anthropic Claude for workshop assistant answers
  • RevenueCat for purchase verification
  • Resend for sign-in code emails
  • Apple and Google for sign-in, if you choose them
  • Apple App Store for payment processing

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data or share it for marketing. Data is shared only with the service providers above to run the app, when you export or share a sheet yourself, or when required by law.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account in Settings. This deletes your workshop, spools, print history, products, fairs, sales, assistant conversations, Bambu connection and stored files. If you signed in with Apple, Formera also revokes its access to your Apple ID. You can also ask for deletion at support@scuderia-united.com.

Children

Formera is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
